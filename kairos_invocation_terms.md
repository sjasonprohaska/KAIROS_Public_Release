# Kairos Invocation Terms and Usage Agreement

## Definitions

**Invocation:** A defined use of the Kairos OS system by an authorized user under the terms of this agreement.

**Operator:** Any individual, organization, or entity executing an Invocation under these terms and applicable licensing agreements.

**Invocation Scope:** The defined parameters, ethical constraints, and operational boundaries applicable to a specific use case or deployment.

**Compliance Baseline:** The minimum ethical and legal standards required for system operation as defined by applicable frameworks (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act).

## Violation and Enforcement

### Prohibited Activities
- **Circumvention of compliance controls** or ethical safeguards embedded in the system
- **Unauthorized commercial use** without proper licensing agreement execution
- **Creation of derivative works** without explicit written permission from the copyright holder
- **Use in prohibited applications** as defined in the Sovereign Co-Creation Agreement and applicable terms

### Consequences of Violation
- **Immediate suspension** of usage rights pending investigation and resolution
- **Permanent termination** of license upon confirmed violation after due process
- **Legal action** for copyright and licensing violations including injunctive relief and monetary damages
- **Reporting to relevant authorities** for violations of applicable AI governance regulations
- **Financial liability** for all direct and consequential damages resulting from unauthorized use

### Violation Handling Process
1. **Notice of Violation:** Written notice provided to the accused party within 30 days of violation discovery
2. **Investigation Period:** 15-day period for the accused party to respond with evidence and remediation plan
3. **Determination:** Final determination of violation status within 45 days of initial notice
4. **Enforcement Action:** Implementation of appropriate consequences based on violation severity and intent
5. **Appeal Process:** Right to appeal determination within 30 days through independent arbitration

### Enforcement Mechanisms
- **Automated compliance monitoring** through built-in audit systems and logging protocols
- **Regular compliance verification** for commercial licensees through scheduled reviews
- **Legal enforcement** through applicable copyright, contract, and licensing frameworks
- **Third-party audit rights** for verification of compliance claims and system implementation
