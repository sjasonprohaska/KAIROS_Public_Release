## Certification and Audit Status

### Self-Assessment Methodology
Kairos OS ∞ includes built-in compliance monitoring and comprehensive audit trail generation designed to support regulatory compliance requirements. All current compliance assessments are based on internal framework analysis against published regulatory standards and industry best practices.

### External Audit Requirements
For enterprise deployment and formal regulatory certification, external audit is required for:
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** - Third-party certification body assessment required for formal certification
- **SOC 2 Type II** - Independent CPA firm audit required for operational compliance attestation
- **Industry-specific regulations** - Sector-specific audits required (healthcare under HIPAA §164.312(b), financial services under SOX §404)

### Compliance Verification Process
1. **Automated monitoring** through clause-based audit system (continuous self-assessment with real-time reporting)
2. **Regular compliance reporting** via digest system (quarterly self-assessment reports with stakeholder distribution)
3. **External audit support** through comprehensive audit trails (annual third-party verification recommended)
4. **Stakeholder transparency** via public documentation and licensing terms (ongoing disclosure and accountability)

### Audit Trail Requirements
- **Retention Period:** 7 years minimum for regulatory compliance documentation and audit evidence
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access with multi-factor authentication and administrator approval for audit log review
- **Integrity Protection:** Cryptographic hashing and digital signatures of audit logs to prevent tampering
- **Export Capability:** Standard formats (JSON, CSV, PDF) for regulatory submission and third-party audit support
